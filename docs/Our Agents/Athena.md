# Athena

## CRM Agent

Athena is our dedicated CRM specialist Agent, connecting with and investigating your CRM to validate your data, fetch details about your contacts, and provide recommendations based on your current ICP

### Tools
- Athena will constantly audit your CRM data quality and let you know what is missing and what can be added.
- Athena can fetch individual details about your contacts, deals, or companies.
- Athena can use the information in your CRM to identify your Ideal Customer Profile (company) and Ideal Customer Persona (target person at that company)
