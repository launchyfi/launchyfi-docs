## Intro

[Seleqt](https://www.seleqt.ai/) positions itself as "the complete sales automation platform for agencies and teams." It combines a 450M+ contact database, enrichment and email finder, AI personalization and lead qualification, multichannel (LinkedIn and email) campaigns, a unified inbox, and analytics, aiming to replace a patchwork of lead‑gen and outreach tools in one workspace.
